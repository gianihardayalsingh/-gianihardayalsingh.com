About

Giani Hardayal Singh is a Sikh religious educator, Gurbani exponent, Gurmat Sangeet practitioner and community servant based in Maryland, United States. He currently serves as a Religious Worker, Granthi and Hazoori Ragi at Gurdwara Sri Guru Singh Sabha of Maryland. His responsibilities include daily religious services, Gurbani recitation and reflection, Shabad Kirtan, Ardas, life-cycle ceremonies, spiritual guidance and community programs.

His work is rooted in the Sikh principles of truthful living, humility, equality, compassion and selfless service. Alongside his religious duties, he supports the next generation through free Saturday classes for children, presenting Sikh teachings, sacred music and moral values in a clear and accessible way.

He also serves as President, Northern India, of Baba Budha Ji International Gurmat Granthi Sabha (Regd.), India, and as Chairman of Guru Kirpa Charitable Trust.

As a Certified Religious Freedom Consultant (CRFC), USIDHR Alumni Ambassador and General Member of the US Institute of Diplomacy and Human Rights, Washington, DC, USA, he advocates human dignity, freedom of belief, interfaith respect and service without discrimination.

Guru Nanak Di Phulwari — Punjabi & Gurmukhi Learning

Teaching Punjabi and Gurmukhi to the children of Guru Nanak Di Phulwari every Saturday is more than a class; it is a humble effort to connect the next generation with their language, Gurbani and Sikh heritage. Beginning with small letters and simple lessons, the aim is to strengthen identity, confidence and love for our shared heritage.

गुरु नानक दी फुलवारी — पंजाबी और गुरमुखी शिक्षा

हर शनिवार गुरु नानक दी फुलवारी के बच्चों को पंजाबी और गुरमुखी सिखाना केवल पढ़ाई नहीं, बल्कि अगली पीढ़ी को अपनी भाषा, गुरबाणी और सिख विरासत से जोड़ने की एक विनम्र सेवा है। छोटे-छोटे अक्षरों से शुरू यह प्रयास बच्चों में पहचान, आत्मविश्वास और अपनी विरासत के प्रति प्रेम को मजबूत करने का माध्यम है।

Sikh Identity, Community & Love for America

Carrying Sikh identity, seva and civic participation together is a meaningful way to express respect for the freedom, diversity and religious liberty found in America. Standing with the community under the message “Sikhs Love America” reflects gratitude for a country where people of different faiths can live openly with their identity while contributing to the wider society.

सिख पहचान, समुदाय और अमेरिका के प्रति सम्मान

सिख पहचान, सेवा और नागरिक भागीदारी को साथ लेकर चलना अमेरिका की स्वतंत्रता, विविधता और धार्मिक स्वतंत्रता के प्रति सम्मान व्यक्त करने का एक अर्थपूर्ण तरीका है। “Sikhs Love America” के संदेश के साथ समुदाय के बीच खड़े होकर उस देश के प्रति कृतज्ञता प्रकट होती है जहाँ अलग-अलग धर्मों के लोग अपनी पहचान के साथ रहते हुए समाज में योगदान दे सकते हैं।
