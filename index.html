About

Giani Hardayal Singh is a Sikh religious educator, Gurbani exponent, Gurmat Sangeet practitioner and community servant based in Maryland, United States. He currently serves as a Religious Worker, Granthi and Hazoori Ragi at Gurdwara Sri Guru Singh Sabha of Maryland. His responsibilities include daily religious services, Gurbani recitation and reflection, Shabad Kirtan, Ardas, life-cycle ceremonies, spiritual guidance and community programs.

His work is rooted in the Sikh principles of truthful living, humility, equality, compassion and selfless service. Alongside his religious duties, he supports the next generation through free Saturday classes for children, presenting Sikh teachings, sacred music and moral values in a clear and accessible way.

He also serves as President, Northern India, of Baba Budha Ji International Gurmat Granthi Sabha (Regd.), India, and as Chairman of Guru Kirpa Charitable Trust.

As a Certified Religious Freedom Consultant (CRFC), USIDHR Alumni Ambassador and General Member of the US Institute of Diplomacy and Human Rights, Washington, DC, USA, he advocates human dignity, freedom of belief, interfaith respect and service without discrimination.
